To ensure a drop-in replacement that functions perfectly with original parts and safeguards astronaut life-support integrity, your design must adhere strictly to the master dimensions, mating surfaces, and clamping mechanisms documented in McDonnell's SEDR 104 manual. [1]

Below are the exact engineering specifications required to manufacture an airtight, structurally sound capsule shell compatible with original Mercury-Redstone hardware.

* * * * *

1\. Master Capsule Dimensions
-----------------------------

The external envelope must perfectly match the original aerodynamics and internal volume constraints of the McDonnell design.

-   Base Max Outer Diameter: 6.2 feet (1.89 meters / 74.5 inches) at the widest point of the aft cylindrical flange.
-   Top (Antenna Fairing) Diameter: 2.0 feet (0.61 meters / 24.0 inches).
-   Capsule Core Length: 10.8 feet (3.3 meters) from the base of the blunt heat shield to the top flat surface of the antenna/parachuting housing.
-   Overall Launch Length: 25.9 feet (7.9 meters) when the Launch Escape Tower is bolted to the top assembly.
-   Total Habitable Volume: 60 cubic feet (1.7 cubic meters) of completely uncompromised, airtight pressurized space. [2, 3, 4]

* * * * *

2\. Rocket Connection & Aft Attachment Mechanisms
-------------------------------------------------

The capsule connects to the Mercury-Redstone Launch Vehicle at its widest diameter using a highly specific tiered mechanical stack. [5]

```
     [ PRESSURIZED CAPSULE SKIN ]
                  ||
 [ MACHINED BASE RING FLANGE (74.5" OD) ]
                  ||
       === [ MARMON CLAMP BAND ] ===   <-- Explosive Bolt Release
                  ||
   [ CAPSULE ADAPTER RING INTERFACE ]
                  ||
      [ REDSTONE MISSILE BOOSTER ]

```

-   The Lower Mating Flange: The capsule's aft pressure bulkhead terminates in a rigid, machined titanium base ring flange. This flange seats directly flush onto the [Mercury-Redstone Capsule Adapter Ring](https://reentrygame.com/officialManuals/MercuryFamiliarizationManual20May1962.pdf), which sits atop the rocket in place of a traditional missile warhead cone. [6]
-   The Marmon Clamp Band Assembly: The capsule is secured to the rocket adapter ring using a heavy-duty, two-piece circumferential V-band (Marmon) clamp. This clamp wraps around the mated lips of both the capsule's base ring and the booster adapter ring.
-   Separation Mechanics: To separate from the booster, the clamp utilizes dual explosive tension bolts. When fired, the clamp band flies outward, allowing three solid-propellant posigrade rockets mounted to the capsule's aft structure to fire for 1 second, cleanly pushing the spacecraft away from the launch vehicle. [7]
-   Airtight Consideration: The pressure envelope ends at the flat aft bulkhead *inside* this outer structural ring. The mating flange itself sits outside the pressure shell, meaning booster separation hardware will never touch or compromise the internal 5.1 psia pure O₂ environment.

* * * * *

3\. Top Section: Parachute/Antenna & Escape Tower Specs
-------------------------------------------------------

The top of the capsule is an exceptionally dense, high-load area that houses recovery parachutes, antennas, and the launch escape tower. [8]

-   The Mating Interface: The extreme top of the capsule features the antenna fairing lower mating flange.
-   Alignment Array: To mount original components correctly, your top layout must feature exactly three index pins and six support clips machined directly into the antenna fairing flange. These must align seamlessly with the three indexed alignment holes and six receiving brackets built into the recovery compartment mating flange.
-   The Launch Escape System (LES) Tower Attachment: The structural legs of the high-thrust escape rocket tower straddle the antenna fairing and bolt directly into the main load-bearing loops of the forward recovery section ring. This tower must endure brutal pull forces to yank the capsule away from a failing rocket on the pad.
-   Tower Jettison Mechanics: The tower attaches via a pyrotechnic clamp ring. At T+2 minutes and 33 seconds (after clearing the dense atmosphere), explosive bolts release this ring, and a small tower-jettison rocket fires to carry the structure away, clearing the path for the deployment of the drogue and main parachutes stored directly beneath it. [6, 7, 9, 10]

* * * * *

4\. Maximizing Airtight Integrity for Astronaut Safety
------------------------------------------------------

To ensure your astronauts can fly safely without relying solely on the secondary protection of an inflated pressure suit: [11]

-   The Double-Seam Solution: As specified by McDonnell Aircraft's master prints, you must overlap two sheets of 0.010-inch titanium. Your resistance seam welding wheels must run along these joints continuously. The overlapping weld nuggets eliminate the microscopic pinholes and structural gaps inherent to standard spot welding, forming a completely solid, gas-impermeable matrix.
-   Feedthrough and Hatch Gaskets: Every penetrative boundary---including the main trapezoidal entry hatch, the double-pane observation window, and electrical/pneumatic umbilical lines---must utilize silicone-elastomeric compression gaskets nested into machined grooves to maintain a absolute seal under vacuum differential pressures.
