SECTION 2: Critical Joint Operation --- 90-Degree Intersecting Seams
------------------------------------------------------------------

When your vertical rolled panels travel up the conical walls of the capsule and intersect with the horizontal ring steps at a 90-degree right angle, they create a high-density, multi-layered metal bulge.

If this junction is left untreated, it will instantly fracture your continuous welding wheels or leave behind microscopic pressure leaks that compromise the crew's shirtsleeve cabin environment.


Mandated 4-Step Shop Floor Execution Sequence
---------------------------------------------

1\. Pre-Flattening the Bulge (Cold-Working)
-------------------------------------------

Before joining the vertical rolled panels to the 1.25" thick heavy base ring forging, your team must locate the exact 90-degree intersection target point. Use a localized hydraulic planar hammer or a precision mechanical press plate to force the multi-layered vertical double seam completely flat at the terminal edge.

This section must be compressed uniformly until its overall height matches the nominal layout profile of a single unrolled sheet layer.

2\. The Horizontal Roll-Over
----------------------------

Once the vertical seam edge is crushed flat, feed the assembly directly into the primary rolling dies. The team must roll the flattened flange section onto the horizontal landing shelf machined into your heavy titanium base adapter.

This mechanical fold locks the vertical panel wall tightly against the horizontal base ring structure before any welding tools ever touch the metal.

3\. Continuous Resistance Seam Fusing
-------------------------------------

Mount the complete flanged assembly onto your automated rotary welding jig. Your continuous resistance seam welding wheels must follow the tracking path programmed inside your digital design layouts.

As the copper wheel rolls along the baseline joint, it applies constant mechanical pressure, ensuring that your sheet layers maintain a completely smooth, continuous orientation before passing over the flattened 90-degree bulge.

4\. The Heavy Over-Weld Pass (The Over-The-Bump Current Ramp)
-------------------------------------------------------------

When the welding wheel rolls straight over the pre-flattened 90-degree junction bump, the sudden density increase shifts the joint's base electrical resistance.

-   Your team must program your welding power controllers to execute a heavy over-weld pass directly over the bump.
-   The system must automatically ramp up the electrical current by 12% to 15% for exactly 240 milliseconds as it traverses the junction point.

This extra thermal energy dumps deep into the multi-layer titanium fold, fusing the crushed metal bulge into a completely solid, gas-impermeable matrix. This eliminates any hidden micro-gaps and guarantees that your ACDelco-style synthetic rubber seals will comfortably lock at their 35.0 PSI airtight baseline, keeping your crew safe without spacesuits under extreme vacuum loads.

SECTION 3: Post-Fabrication Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)
---------------------------------------------------------

1.  X-Ray Radiography Audit: Every 90-degree intersecting over-weld point must pass a full cross-sectional X-ray analysis to confirm complete material fusion and verify that zero interior gas pockets or hidden fissures formed during the current ramp phase.
2.  Helium Leak Detection Ring Pass: Seal the finished titanium canister inside your vacuum testing envelope. The internal space must maintain a strict zero-leak profile when pressurized under a 1-atmosphere differential loop.

If any pressure drop is caught by the sensor networks, the factory line software will stop the run and trigger an immediate safe-state reset pass to protect your titanium components from irreversible structural warping.
